# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are inconsistent with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior through a private maintainer contact option provided by the repository. If no private conduct channel is available, open a minimal issue asking a maintainer to establish private contact and do not include sensitive details. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent removal from the project community.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider context, impact, and recurrence when deciding on a response. Responses may include a private correction, a warning, temporary restriction from project interaction, or a permanent ban for repeated or severe violations.
